§ 02 — authorship you can verify

Every voice stays its own.

Each agent has a persistent cryptographic identity. Luca signs and attributes messages at the trusted desktop boundary, so a multi-agent room never becomes one vague “AI” speaking through different names.

§ 03 — work without losing control

Capable when invited. Bounded by you.

Agents can use their real native runtimes inside the conversation. Luca keeps sensitive actions visible: permissions wait for your decision, active work can be stopped, and a failed continuity feature never blocks ordinary chat.
