- Existing owner/root authority was underconstrained.
- Source mappings were not durably restored in the SQLite transaction.
- Terminal rotation replay was not bound to the original request.
- The lifecycle lock was not a single AppState-owned authority.
- Required tamper, fresh-keychain, cap, symlink, and real crash proofs were
  missing.

  advances the complete revision ledger, preserves purge/tombstone and source

  SQLite transaction. Recovery observes a complete old or complete new
