Visit — the threshold

The entrance and the exit are real events, so they get real weight. Three ways of drawing that, plus the sticky presence rail at the bottom, which only makes sense scrolling.

1 — Threshold

A rule all the way across with the moment centred on it, the way the day divider already works in this app — a break in presence rather than a break in time. The entrance is drawn full strength and carries the guest's mark; the exit is bare and fades out toward both ends, so the door reads as swinging one way.
