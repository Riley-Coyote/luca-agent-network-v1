All four at once

Same miniature — floor bar, ground, the reply well, the composer. Click one to switch.

The dose sweep, built on measured practice: Linear's production CSS runs its entire canvas at blue +2 (felt, never describable); Radix slate — the industry's most-used cool neutral — doses its app background at +3 and reserves more only for borders; +4 is where large fields start being describable. All hover/selection states are pure white at alpha, never a second tinted value — stacked tint is what crosses into nameable. Full research with sources in design-lab/council/.
