Council — neutral

All four options share the same taller staircase — the fix for "flat and muddled" (each surface now clearly sits on the one below). They differ only in temperature: how the greys lean, dosed to stay below the point where you could name a color. The reply well and composer below are the shipped anatomy on each candidate.

Temperature proof: the candidate's ground beside a pure-neutral twin at the same brightness. If you can see the seam between them, the lean exists; if either half looks like "a color" on its own, the dose is wrong.

All four at once

Same miniature — floor bar, ground, the reply well, the composer. Click one to switch.

All four ladders keep identical brightness steps (the council's widened staircase); only the lean differs, held constant per altitude as an offset rule — never a percentage, which is the mistake that made an earlier attempt visibly blue. Skylight is the colorist's recommendation: the cool of its depths and the warmth of its raised surfaces read as light, not color, and the cream text is lit by the same source as the surfaces it sits on.
