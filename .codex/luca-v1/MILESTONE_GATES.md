# Milestone Gates

## Universal gate format

Every gate record contains candidate commit, dependency versions, topology,
test logs, recordings/screenshots where applicable, redacted traces, performance
measurements, independent reviewer, findings and evidence hashes.

## G0 - Source truth and feasibility

- untouched Buzz baseline build/test evidence;
- exact immutable baseline and license/branding decision;
- native lifecycle/Orphan-Job verdict;
- exact Buzz/Luca/Mnemos/Polyphonic reuse coordinates;
- zero-write and mutation-control proof;
- safe fixture plan and baseline budgets;
- final decision deltas and full task graph.

Product code may begin only after G0 passes. The Keep Thinking sub-gate failed
and the feature is removed; this does not block the simplified product.

## G1 - Personal agent home

- P1 persistent-resident behavior;
- continuity-absent conversation harness;
- clean-profile native shell and public-brand audit;
- no conductor path or organization-first blocker;
- a real native clean profile automatically provisions/selects its internal
  personal-home tenancy and never exposes Buzz community/workspace setup;
- upstream messaging regression suite;
- resident registry/setup, real ACP provider and owner-recovery proof;
- installed-app threads, attachments/media and search smoke;
- model/ACP descendant environment proves no resident secret or broker
  capability.

## G2 - Continuity Capsule

- P2 fresh-session identity and fast-head freshness;
- P3 authority isolation and tamper rejection;
- structured signing broker, NIP-AE vectors, cache/archive and managed-
  coordinator conflict/concurrency/crash tests;
- secret/artifact scan;
- independent cryptography/authority review.

## G3 - Universal brain

- P6 ingestion, paired brain answer and isolation;
- complete visibility/egress truth table;
- exact outbound remote payload capture;
- byte/logical zero-write proof including wrong-project regression;
- all degradation states plus conversation survival;
- IPC spoof/replay and path-redaction tests;
- packaged sidecar works without ambient Python;
- person/project relevance and legacy policy-overlay classification proofs;
- independent privacy/architecture review.

## G4 - Guarded rooms

- P7 three-resident direct room proof;
- recursive cap, deadline, spend, replay and cancellation tests;
- per-responder memory reauthorization and cross-agent capture;
- no hidden conductor or child background work;
- independent multi-agent safety review.

## G5 - Post-turn continuity

- P4 qualification, abstention, commit and immediate freshness;
- response succeeds under checkpoint faults;
- duplicate and crash-point recovery;
- expected-head/writer-epoch conflict and coordinator replay;
- health relative to latest qualifying input;
- independent exactly-once review.

## G6 - Resident backup and restore

- P5 clean same-owner second-environment proof;
- exact resident public key and Capsule-head comparison;
- age dependency/license and passphrase policy review;
- exact original signed event IDs, active-writer transfer and journal recovery;
- tamper, owner mismatch, conflict and active-partial-resident rejection;
- no brain/transcript/credential/path in bundle;
- independent key-custody and claim review.

## G8 - Integrated design-partner candidate

- P1-P7 and S1-S6 evidence closed against one installed build;
- clean install/recovery, native window/accessibility and performance matrix;
- uninterrupted main demo and backup/restore recording;
- the installed app used by every G8 recording and smoke has the identical
  SHA-256 recorded in D02's verified signed-bundle manifest; its bundle ID,
  public signing identity/team, architecture and entitlements hash match, and
  `codesign --verify --deep --strict` plus applicable Gatekeeper assessment are
  captured in secret-safe evidence;
- full secret/artifact/egress scan;
- upstream notices and public rebrand audit;
- no unresolved P0/P1 and final known-limit/claim audit.

Public release remains human-gated on non-author seven-day use of the identical
candidate build.
