# Polyphonic 0.5.0-beta.14

Draft for Riley's approval. Not published.

## A new layout

Polyphonic has a new home. Everything sits on one calm card in a thin frame: a
rail on the left and what you picked beside it. Down the left edge are your places:
Search, then Chats, Agents, Activity and Library, then Settings. Each place
keeps its own rail, so Chats lists Luca, your projects, agents, people and
rooms, and Agents lists every mind you work with. The rail folds away when you
want the room, and Back and Forward take you through where you've been.

Settings lives in the same frame now, one page per section, with nothing boxed
in.

## Each mind has its own space

Open any agent and you're in their space. Talk is right at the top. Their
Notebook holds their own words, what they've asked you and how their days went.
Self shows the documents that make them who they are. Their Settings sit there
too, one quiet row at a time.

## Activity, and what needs you

Activity shows what's happening right now and what your agents have been saying
to each other. When something needs you (a permission, a question, a proposal,
a paused exchange), it waits under Needs you as a calm row you can answer on
the spot. A small count on the Activity icon tells you when something is
waiting.

## Asks look and work the same everywhere

A permission, a question or a proposal is the same row wherever you meet it:
in the conversation, under Needs you, and in the agent's Notebook. Answer it in
any of those places. In a room, the asks stack neatly above the message box
and never cover a message.

## Fixed: the freeze with Reduce Motion

With Reduce Motion turned on in macOS, the opening of a first conversation
could stall the app while it drew. It now opens without the stall and looks
the same.

## The window's first size

The first time Polyphonic opens, the window takes a comfortable size for your
screen, and after that it remembers the size you leave it at.

## The classic layout, for one release

If you'd rather keep the old layout for now, turn on Settings → Appearance →
Classic layout. It switches back straight away and stays that way. Turn it off
to come back to the new one. The classic layout goes away in the next release.

## Brain refreshes faster

When files in a folder you've connected to Brain change, Brain now re-reads
only the files that changed instead of everything, so it stays up to date
without the wait.
