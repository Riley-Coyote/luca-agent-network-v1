# Polyphonic 0.5.0-beta.14

Draft for Riley's approval. Not published.

## A new layout

Polyphonic has a new home. Everything sits on one calm card in a thin frame: a
rail on the left and what you picked beside it. Down the left edge are your
places: Search, then Chats, Agents, Activity, Library and Brain, then Settings. The
rail folds away when you want the room, and Back and Forward take you through
where you've been.

## Chats: what needs you, Luca, and the work

Chats starts with what only you can do. When an agent is waiting on you (a
permission, a question, a proposal), Needs you sits at the very top with a
count, and you can answer right there.

Below it is Luca, one ongoing conversation. Then your projects, your agents and
your apps, like Claude Code and Codex. When Luca hands work to one of them, it
shows under whoever is doing it while they work, so you can see at a glance
who's on what. Every agent and app has a + to start a conversation of your own.

A project's conversations sit directly beneath it. Section labels and icons
make the rail easier to scan, without opening a second column to reach a chat.

## A living mark for each agent

Each agent has a branching mark of their own, with quiet movement at rest and a
different rhythm while they work. Their mark stays with them when you change
their model or runtime. Reduce Motion keeps it still.

## Useful agents, without a crowded rail

Luca, Anima and Vektor are there to start with. Browse the starter agents to add
research, writing, design or review specialists, each with practical example
requests. Other agents appear in Chats when you message them or Luca puts them
to work, and stay there until you choose to hide them.

## Agent conversations that stay together

Luca and another agent share one ongoing inbox instead of creating a new room
for every exchange. Individual tasks stay distinct inside it. Luca can wake a
configured agent, send a request and bring the reply back to your conversation.
No project is created and no drawer opens on its own. Click the task card to
open its chat beside yours, reply there, and return to Luca without losing your
place. Closing the chat does not stop the work.

A delegated agent can work through a task's steps with the tools its runtime
provides; it is not limited to one action per message. Runtime approvals and
bounded work limits still apply.

## One familiar message box

The same solid, uncluttered composer now appears in the main conversation and
mini chat. A new conversation starts with a calmer view, and the message box
keeps its shape and controls consistent as you move between chats.

## Mini chat, close at hand

Mini chat keeps its glass surface, with smoother opening and closing. Resize
it to suit what you're doing; it remembers the size you leave it at.

## An app's work, as it happens

Ask Luca to hand work to connected Codex or Claude Code, including continuing
an existing session where the harness supports it. Progress and results come
back to Polyphonic, with questions and blockers kept visible.

Open a task under Claude Code or Codex to see the work itself: what it's doing
right now, every step so far, what came back when it finished, and a button to
open the session in the app.

## A companion that can keep up

Ask Luca to follow your Claude Code or Codex work. Polyphonic finds the usual
local profiles for you and asks for approval before using new or changed
session activity in your conversation. No folder picking is needed for the
normal setup, and following does not import your old history.

Note keeping and background work are separate choices. You can review or
pause access from the agent's Settings as well as through chat. When Luca
visibly controls the app, a soft border and a Stop button make it clear.

Luca can guide this setup through your conversation, including a small
recent-work sample after approval. Useful source-linked notes can be reviewed,
corrected or forgotten. Optional proactive returns respect your quiet hours
and daily limit; work runs while the app is available, not while the Mac sleeps.

## Pages that say what they are

Every page now opens with its name and one line about how things stand, and
its contents sit on quiet panels, so a page reads at a glance.

## Each mind has its own space

Open any agent and you're in their space. Talk is right at the top. Their
Notebook holds their days in order: what they did for you, their own work,
their journal. Self shows the documents that make them who they are, and their
Settings sit there too. On a wide window, the side shows how they run and who
they work with.

Clearer headings and subtle dividing lines separate each part. Settings are
open and visible instead of hidden behind a series of dropdowns.

## Activity is the record

Activity now keeps the record of everything your agents and apps did, newest
first, with each agent's own signed record beside it.

## Asks look and work the same everywhere

A permission, a question or a proposal is the same wherever you meet it: in the
conversation, under Needs you, and in the agent's Notebook. Answer it in any of
those places. In a room, the asks stack neatly above the message box and never
cover a message.

## Fixed: the freeze with Reduce Motion

With Reduce Motion turned on in macOS, the opening of a first conversation
could stall the app while it drew. It now opens without the stall and looks
the same.

## The window's first size

The first time Polyphonic opens, the window takes a comfortable size for your
screen, and after that it remembers the size you leave it at.

## The classic layout, for one release

If you'd rather keep the old layout for now, turn on Settings → Appearance →
Classic layout. It switches back straight away and stays that way. Turn it off
to come back to the new one. The classic layout goes away in the next release.

## Brain refreshes faster

When files in a folder you've connected to Brain change, Brain now re-reads
only the files that changed instead of everything, so it stays up to date
without the wait.
