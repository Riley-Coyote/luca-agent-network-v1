expression
the eyes follow your cursor, glance on their own, and look at whichever agent is working. white is the resting truth. the only colour the face ever wears is cold — and only while it reads your screen.

the expression system

one parametric eye pair on a phosphor lattice: width, openness, upper-lid angle, lower crescent, skew, pupil. every expression is a point in that space, so the face morphs between states instead of swapping frames, and the phosphor leaves a breath of trail behind every move.

v2: taller, softer eye shape on a finer lattice; an angled upper lid (so it can look uncertain or intent); a round pupil that sits low and follows the gaze, with a catchlight; two-speed gaze (fast saccade, slow settle); and a two-timescale phosphor — a bright front over a slow afterglow.

the marks

real brand marks, nominative, small — the ring carries the state. openai, anthropic and grok are the vendors' own vectors; hermes and openclaw are placeholders until their files land.

three tiers

compact — the hardware notch: eyes only. when something's running, the notch grows down one row and the rings sit beneath the eyes. island — the face, then who's working, then one line. open — the mini app: face, name and state, agents, the thread, a composer. eyes always above; indicators always below.
