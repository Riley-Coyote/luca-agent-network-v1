the eyes follow your cursor, glance on their own, and look at whichever agent is working. white is the resting truth. the only colour the face ever wears is cold — and only while it reads your screen.

the expression system

one parametric eye pair on a phosphor lattice: width, openness, upper-lid angle, lower crescent, skew, pupil. every expression is a point in that space, so the face morphs between states instead of swapping frames, and the phosphor leaves a breath of trail behind every move.

what's new over the first studies: an angled upper lid (so it can look uncertain or intent, not just open/closed), a catchlight inside the pupil (one lit cell — it's what makes eyes read as wet and awake), two-speed gaze (fast saccade, slow settle), and a two-timescale phosphor — a bright front over a slow afterglow.

the marks

harnesses are shown nominatively, monochrome, small. the ring carries the state, not the logo — colour stays a signal.

three tiers

compact — the hardware notch. eyes, and when something's running, tiny rings at the trailing edge. island — a hover or a wake: the face, who's working, one line. open — the mini app: agents, the thread with your agent, a composer. everything you need in the corner of your eye, nothing you don't.
