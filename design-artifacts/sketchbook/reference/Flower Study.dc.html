Where the detail comes from
Petal edges are two strokes that overshoot at the base and leave the tip open, so the contour never closes cleanly. Each petal gets its own wash laid in beads across the axis, heaviest at the throat, and its creases run under the wash rather than over it. Value is set by one key light from the upper left: a petal turned into it takes more pigment, one turned away is left nearer the bare paper. And because the ink here is light on black, the eraser draws shadow rather than highlight — it is used for the dark gap a front petal throws onto the one behind it, while the rim light is chalk going on.
Limits worth knowing
The wash still has a ceiling, so every true dark is chalk or charcoal. There is no colour in the system and no cast light beyond a single upper-left key. Petal count, ring twist and stem lean are parameters — say a number and it redraws.
