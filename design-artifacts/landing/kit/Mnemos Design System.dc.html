Mnemos Research · Polyphonic · the continuity layer

One machine, two housings, three materials.

The work is a memory system for minds that would otherwise start from nothing. It should look like an instrument built by people who measure things — quiet, exact, and unembarrassed about being a machine.

This is the whole system: tokens, the lattice, materials, parts, motion, and the four formats it has to survive — web, deck, social, app. Restraint is the strategy. Almost everything here is a rule about what not to add.

material one
Housing

Structure, chrome, controls, prose. Opaque, monochrome, still. It holds things and claims nothing.

material two
Glass

Anything that floats above the page: overlays, menus, the HUD. Translucent, blurred, edge-lit by a single hairline.

material three
DISPLAY

Content that is alive. A dot lattice holding charge, identical at noon and midnight. The only material allowed to emit — and the only image the brand has.

Signal

One red, two calibrations. #E03C2F on dark, #CE2E1C on light, because a bright red vibrates on white. It marks live, recording, destructive and error — states, never objects. Maximum one signal event per screen.

Phosphor

Identity, emitted. Each mind in the chorus holds one hue, and it only ever appears as light on glass at dot scale — an emblem, a ring, a trace. Phosphor never touches housing, and housing never borrows a phosphor hue.

Planes

Bone, clay, moss. Marketing only, and only as a full-bleed field behind type — never a border, a button, a chart series or an icon. One plane per page, and the page must still read monochrome without it.

03

Three voices

Instrument Sans is the person talking. Fragment Mono is the machine stating a fact. Doto is the display itself speaking — and it is rationed, because that is what keeps it meaning something.

Instrument Sans · 400 / 500 / 600

Continuity costs a file on a laptop.

Weight 500 above 1.2rem, 600 for small titles and chrome, 400 for prose. Tracking is a function of size: −.042em at hero, −.022em at title, 0 at body. Never letterspace the sans positively.

Fragment Mono · 400

543 to 1 · 350 tests · 13 of 13

Labels, figures, provenance, code, tabular numerals. Uppercase at .14–.18em when it is a label; sentence case at 0 when it is data. Never above 13px, and never for prose.

Doto · 600 / 900 · the display voice

memory VERIFIED

0123456789

The same dots as the signal behind it. Three legal uses, and no fourth: inside a display well; exactly one word of a headline, where the display is the thing speaking; and live readouts — counters, clocks, a number that is changing.

One instance per view. Never for prose, never for a label, never for a word that is not about something happening now.

Set, and drawn the fourth register · engine only

Doto is set — a webfont, in housing, at title scale, once. Above that scale the dot voice is drawn: the engine renders the phrase cell by cell on its own 5×7 face, at the panel's real dot pitch, and it arrives by dithering in rather than fading.

Drawn type can carry a whole page — a cover, a social post, a slide with one line on it. It is the only thing in the system permitted to be that large, and it can only ever appear on glass. A drawn headline in housing is not a headline, it is a mistake.

04

Space and radius

8px baseline · small radii · hard corners on purpose

Radii are small enough to read as machined rather than soft — 6, 10, 14 — and the hard 90° corner is kept as a deliberate texture rather than banished. Both registers in one page is the whole effect; either one alone is a different brand.

6px · control
Buttons, inputs, chips, segmented, swatches.
10px · panel
Cards, wells, anything holding other things.
14px · surface
Glass that floats: modals, menus, HUD, notch.
999px · pill
Status, tags, avatars, toggles. Nothing larger.
0 · square
Full-bleed bands and planes, catalog grids, table rules.

Where square is right

Three places, and it should feel like a decision each time: anything that runs edge to edge, a catalog of specimens set on a 1px hairline grid, and the rules of a data table. Everything else takes a radius. A page of nothing but square corners reads as a terminal emulator; a page of nothing but soft ones reads as a consumer app.

Nesting and rhythm

An inner radius equals the outer minus the padding between them: a 10px panel with 4px padding holds 6px children.

06

Round dots on a lattice you can see

One grid underneath everything: the mark, the identity emblems, the display glyphs, the avatars. Odd-numbered, square, round-dotted, with the unlit dots left visible — they are not background, they are the field the mark is printed on, and leaving them there is what makes an emblem read as switched on rather than drawn.

the mark · 7 × 7 · shipped
An M, printed on its field

Clear space is one dot pitch on every side. Lit dots at full phosphor, unlit at #1F211F. Below 16px the unlit field is dropped and only the lit dots remain, because at that size the lattice stops being legible and starts being grain.

pitch 1.6× dot · weight 34% · mirrored on one axis
Resolution same seed · same algorithm · finer lattice

An identity emblem is generated from a seed, not drawn. The same seed at a finer lattice gives a denser emblem with more internal structure — more like a seal, less like a pixel. Seven is the mark; nine is the app avatar; anything above thirteen is for print and large formats only.

The chorus

Every mind that speaks in Polyphonic gets an emblem from the same generator and one phosphor hue. This is the only place identity colour exists, and it exists at dot scale on glass — never as a coloured chip, a coloured name, or a coloured border in the housing.

Because the emblem is computed from the name, it cannot be redrawn wrongly and it cannot collide by accident. A new member arrives with a mark already made.

The display

five scenes · all computed · none decorative

The signature of the brand, and the one thing nobody else has. A dot lattice whose charge buffer persists between frames and is multiplied down rather than erased, so nothing ever cuts to black — it goes out. Load dot-display.js, put a canvas in a well, name a scene.

The editorial spread well left, argument right · alternate down the page
display · learned, then lost told · 01
§ 02 — what it costs

It isn't that you repeat yourself. It's that it can't learn.

A long-form page is this spread, repeated and alternated: one well, one argument, one thing to notice. The well carries what the paragraph cannot say — and the corner labels are the only annotation the layout gets.

Light housing for this format. The glass stays black, so the page has exactly one dark object per beat and the eye goes there.

Data, on glass and off it

A live series belongs on glass, drawn in dots. A finished figure belongs in housing, drawn as hairlines. Either way: one series in ink, signal only on the current value, tabular numerals, a single baseline rule and no gridlines. Direct labels, never a legend.

The caption band: well labels occupy the first nine dot rows. Scenes start their content at DotDisplay.CAP and reserve their own footer rows. Every collision ever produced in this language came from ignoring that.
